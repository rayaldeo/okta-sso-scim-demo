Okta SSO + SCIM provisioning demo

This app uses Okta as its identity provider:

  • Sign-in with SAML 2.0 or OpenID Connect (authorization code + PKCE).
  • Provisioning: Okta pushes users and group memberships here over SCIM 2.0.
  • Access is decided server-side from Okta group membership: admin or viewer.
  • Audit: sign-ins, provisioning and deprovisioning events are logged.
